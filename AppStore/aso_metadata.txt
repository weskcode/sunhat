DESCRIPTION (4000 char max, NOT indexed for search)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Stop checking the forecast, let SunHat watch the weather for you. Set reminders that trigger automatically when temperature, sky, and forecast conditions match your plans. Perfect for runners, gardeners, beach lovers, and anyone who lives by the weather.

WEATHER-TRIGGERED REMINDERS
Set reminders that fire based on real weather conditions instead of fixed times. Going for a morning run? SunHat alerts you when the temperature hits your ideal range. Planning a beach day? Get notified when it's sunny and warm enough. Need to water the garden? SunHat skips the reminder when rain is in the forecast.

SMART TEMPERATURE TRIGGERS
Define precise temperature conditions with range or exact triggers. Set comfortable windows like 55-70 F for running, or above 80 F for pool days. SunHat continuously monitors weather data and triggers your reminder the moment conditions align.

REAL-TIME WEATHER MONITORING
SunHat checks weather conditions in the background so you never miss the perfect moment. View current temperature, humidity, wind speed, and hourly forecasts right from your dashboard. Weather data is refreshed automatically using Apple WeatherKit.

LOCATION-AWARE ALERTS
Get weather data for your exact location or choose any city manually. SunHat uses your location only to fetch local forecasts from enabled weather providers.

POWERFUL NOTIFICATION CONTROLS
- Quiet Hours, silence notifications during sleep (e.g., 10 PM - 7 AM)
- Daily Limits, cap the number of alerts per day
- Smart Scheduling, SunHat picks the best time to notify you based on your preferences

FEATURES AT A GLANCE
- Weather-based reminders triggered by temperature, sky conditions, and forecasts
- Temperature range and exact-match triggers
- Background weather monitoring with automatic refresh
- Hourly forecast and current conditions dashboard
- Location-aware weather data with manual city selection
- Quiet hours and daily notification limits
- Beautiful Liquid Glass interface designed for iOS 26
- Privacy-first, reminders and preferences stay on your device
- No account required, works instantly after download

BUILT FOR iOS 26
SunHat is designed from the ground up for iOS 26 with Apple's Liquid Glass design language. Every surface, button, and card uses native glass effects for a premium, translucent experience that feels right at home on your iPhone.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Your location is used only to fetch weather data and is never stored on our servers. SunHat requires no account and collects no personal analytics. Your reminders and preferences stay on your device.

Download SunHat and start getting reminders that actually match the weather.
