# Product Overview

## TempTrigger (Hatti)

**Tagline:** "Smart reminders triggered by weather, not time"

TempTrigger is a location-aware iOS reminder app that intelligently triggers notifications based on temperature conditions, weather patterns, and seasonal transitions rather than traditional calendar scheduling. The app revolutionizes how people plan weather-dependent activities by creating the world's first temperature-intelligent reminder system.

### Core Value Proposition
- Weather-triggered reminders instead of time-based alerts
- Intelligent pattern recognition for optimal activity conditions
- Location-aware weather monitoring with background processing
- Sophisticated trigger conditions (exact temperature, ranges, trends, seasonal markers)

### Target Users
- Outdoor enthusiasts (hikers, runners, cyclists)
- Gardening community (home gardeners, farmers, landscapers)
- Home maintenance property owners
- Health-conscious individuals with weather-sensitive conditions

### Key Features
- **Advanced Temperature Triggers**: Exact matching, ranges, "feels like" integration
- **Pattern Recognition**: Consecutive day patterns, seasonal transitions
- **Smart Notifications**: Context-aware timing and rich content
- **Natural Language Input**: "Remind me to plant tomatoes when it's consistently above 50°F"
- **Multiple Locations**: Monitor different locations for various activities
- **Background Processing**: Continuous weather monitoring with battery optimization

### App Categories
The app supports various reminder categories including outdoor activities, gardening, exercise, home maintenance, travel, health, sports, work, seasonal tasks, and emergency preparedness.
